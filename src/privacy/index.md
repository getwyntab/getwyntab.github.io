# Privacy policy

WYNTab is an Open Source browser extension.
This SERVICE is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

## Privacy First

WYNTab is designed to be privacy-respecting. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information.

## Information Collection and Use

All data managed by WYNTab, including your custom HTML templates and settings, is stored **locally** in your browser using the `chrome.storage.local` API. 

*   **No Server Sync**: WYNTab does not have a server-side component and does not sync your data to any external cloud services (unless you manually export and import backups).
*   **No Analytics**: We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase, or any other tracking scripts in the extension.
*   **No Crash Reporting**: We do not collect crash reports or usage statistics.

## External Links & Resources

If you include external images or resources (like CSS frameworks) in your custom HTML templates, your browser will make requests to those external servers. These requests are governed by the privacy policies of those third-party providers.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update our Privacy Policy. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to reach out to us via our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/getwyntab/wyntab).
