# Backups & Data Portability

WYNTab stores all your custom templates and settings locally in your browser. To ensure you never lose your creations, we provide a robust backup system.

## Exporting a Backup

1.  Open the **Dashboard** and go to **Settings** (or the **Custom Library** if you have templates).
2.  Click **Export Backup**.
3.  A `.json` file containing all your custom templates and your current active selection will be downloaded.

## Importing a Backup

1.  Open the **Dashboard** and go to **Settings**.
2.  Click **Import JSON**.
3.  Select a previously exported WYNTab backup file.
4.  Your library will be restored, and your active template will be updated.

## Local Storage

WYNTab uses the `unlimitedStorage` permission to handle large HTML templates. Your data never leaves your machine and is never transmitted to external servers.
