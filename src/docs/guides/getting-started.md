# Getting Started

WYNTab (Write Your NewTab) is a browser extension that enables full customization of the New Tab page by activating custom HTML templates.

## Installation

1.  Visit the [Download Page](/download/) and choose your browser.
2.  Click **Add to Chrome** (or the equivalent button for your browser).
3.  Once installed, WYNTab will automatically open an onboarding page.

## Using the Dashboard

The Dashboard is the central hub for managing your templates. You can access it by clicking the WYNTab icon in your browser's extension toolbar.

### Activating a Template

1.  Open the **Built-in Gallery** tab in the Dashboard.
2.  Hover over a template you like.
3.  Click the **Zap (Activate)** icon.
4.  Open a new tab to see your new interface!

### Creating Your Own

1.  Switch to the **Custom Library** tab.
2.  Click **Create Live** to open the integrated editor with a starter template.
3.  Modify the HTML and CSS to your liking.
4.  Click **Save Changes** to add it to your library.
